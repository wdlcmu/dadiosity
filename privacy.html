Privacy

The short version: We collect your email for daily lessons.

What joining collects

The signup form sends only your email address.

The save code adds the join time.

It also adds one source label: holding-page.

Your address is the key for that record.

Where it is saved

The save code writes to Cloudflare KV.

If it is not connected, nothing is saved.

The signup list also sits in Resend.

Resend is the email tool built for lessons.

Once its key is set, signups go there.

It tracks which lesson comes next.

The signup code sets no end date.

Your network address only limits signup tries.

That try count is kept for one hour.

It is not saved in the signup record.

What it is for

Your email joins the Dadiosity signup list.

The list is for one lesson each day.

Each lesson comes by email.

Sending is built, but has not started.

No spam. No ads. We never sell your email.

What we do not collect

The form asks for no name.

It asks for no phone or payment data.

What we count

This site runs two counting scripts.

PostHog counts views, shares, joins, and form starts.

Cloudflare counts visits in browsers and servers.

PostHog keeps an anonymous ID for counting.

The ID sits in a cookie and storage.

This site keeps two small marks too.

One notes a share arrival.

One notes that you joined.

None holds your email address.

Your email is never sent to PostHog.

It does not record your screen.

Leaving and removal

Each lesson will carry a stop link.
