Dadiosity Daily, Lesson 13: Restaurants

Make the wait part of the meal

The idea: A restaurant meal is mostly waiting. And waiting is the hard part. So the wait is not the gap before the meal. The wait is part of the meal.

Tonight's move: Before the wait starts. Give her one real choice about dinner. One small pick that is truly hers. A kid with a choice is a host. Not a prisoner of the chair.

Tonight's play: At home, build a tiny pretend restaurant. Blocks for tables, a blanket over the door. She seats the stuffed animals. She takes your order in scribbles. She brings one pretend dish at a time. Pay with counted thank-yous and a big tip.

Why it works: Waiting feels long. When nothing in it is yours. A choice and a small job make the minutes hers. And time she owns moves faster.
