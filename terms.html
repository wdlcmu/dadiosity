What Dadiosity is

Dadiosity is for daily dad lessons by email.

It is for dads of little kids.

The home page says: Coming soon.

First lesson at launch, then one a day.

What lessons are

Lessons give general ideas for parents.

Lessons are not medical advice.

Lessons are not mental health advice.

Lessons are not safety advice.

They are not advice for one specific child.

Use your own judgment with your child.

Joining by email

The signup form sends only your email address.

The save code adds the join time.

It also adds one source label: holding-page.

Your address is the key for that record.

The save code writes to Cloudflare KV.

If that store is not connected, no signup is saved.

On success, joining shows: You are in.

It also shows: First lesson comes at launch.

Nothing is sent from the signup code.

No lesson email is sent now.
