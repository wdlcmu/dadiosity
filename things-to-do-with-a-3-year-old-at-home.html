Things to do with a 3-year-old at home

It is 4 p.m. She is bored, you are tired, and every toy is already out. You do not need a plan or new toys. Pick one thing below and give it ten minutes. Every one uses what you already have at home.

  1. Roll a ball on the floor. Sit on the floor with a soft ball. Roll it to her and wait. When she rolls it back, cheer like it is a big win. Let the game stay slow and silly.
  2. Make faces in a mirror. Stand at a mirror together. Make a happy face, a sad face, and a mad face. Name each one out loud. Let her pick the next face for you to make.
  3. Send a bear down a slide. Build a slide out of books. Send a stuffed bear down it. Count down out loud together. Clap when the bear lands and the turn ends.
  4. Talk through a block tower. Stack blocks together and say what you see in short lines. Red block on top. Big block falls down. Laugh, build it again, and keep the words just as plain as that.
  5. Pick hats for each other. Line up two hats or two scarves. Let her pick one for you and one for her. Wear the pick with pride. Then pick for her stuffed bear.
  6. Play the freeze game. Move to music or claps, then freeze when the sound stops. Take turns starting and stopping the sound. Laugh when you freeze mid step.
  7. Run a post office. Set up a post office with paper and a box. She stamps each card and drops it in the box. You write one card to her bear and thank her for the mail.
  8. Hunt for hidden toys. Hide three soft toys in one room. Hunt for them slow, one by one. When she finds one, stop and cheer the find like it is gold.
  9. Clean up while one song plays. Pick one song you like. Race toys back to their home box before the song ends. Stop when the song stops, and cheer for what got done.
  10. Open lids and jars. Put a few safe lids and jars on the floor. Let her open and close each one at her own pace. You hold the jar still. She does the turning.
  11. Play the whisper game. Whisper a word for her to guess, then let her whisper one for you. Go back and forth, hushed and giggling, till words run out.
  12. Dance like the floor is hot. Put on one fast song and dance. Freeze when it stops, fall down laughing, then get up and go again till the song ends.

The one rule that makes all of these work

Let her lead. Follow her play idea for ten minutes without leading or fixing it. Ask what happens next, then do that. Her game, her pace. It is a full Dadiosity lesson on how to play with a toddler.
