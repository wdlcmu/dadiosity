What can I do with my 3-year-old today?

Start with ten minutes of her leading the play. Then keep the day simple: one real job for her, one walk where you stop for what she finds, and the same three steps at bedtime. Two full lessons are on this page. Each takes about a minute to read.

Right now: let her lead

Dadiosity Daily, Lesson 22: Follow Her Lead in Play

Follow her lead

The idea: Most play has a grown up plan under it. You pick the game, set the rules, and fix what drifts. When she leads, you get to see how her mind works.

Tonight's move: Tonight, for ten minutes, follow her play idea just as she gives it. Do not add rules, fix the plot, or swap in a new game. Ask what happens next, then do that.

Tonight's play: Build a town from blocks and soft toys. She picks who lives there and where roads go. You move the cars where she sends them and say the lines she gives you.

Why it works: Led play tells her her ideas can hold a room. She thinks in steps and tries them out loud. You learn what she loves by staying inside her plan.

Core move: Follow her play idea for ten minutes without leading or fixing it.

A plain plan for the rest of today

  • Morning: Sit with her for the first five minutes of the day. Hold her, talk soft, and ask for nothing yet.
  • Midday: Give her one real job she can finish. Carry the napkins to the table. Put the spoons by each plate. Thank her for the work, by name.
  • On a walk: Stop for one thing she finds. A leaf, a bug, a stone. Crouch down and look with her for a minute.
  • Before dinner: Move and shake with her for two minutes. Jump, spin, march. End slow with three deep breaths, then walk to the table.
  • At the table: Keep your phone in another room for the whole meal.
  • Bedtime: The same three steps, in the same order. The lesson below shows how.

End the day: same steps at bedtime

Dadiosity Daily, Lesson 6: Same Steps at Bedtime

Keep the same three steps

The idea: Bedtime goes better when the end of the day feels known. Same steps, in the same order, tell her body what comes next. New tricks each night do the opposite.

Tonight's move: Pick three steps tonight and keep the order fixed. Bath, book, then song works well. Say each step as you start it. Do not add a fourth step when she asks for more.

Tonight's play: Put a stuffed bear to bed before her turn comes. A bath in a toy tub, one short book, one short song. Use the same order you will use for her.

Why it works: A known order feels safe at night. Safe kids settle with less fight in them. She can rest because she knows the road home to sleep.

Core move: Keep the same three bedtime steps in the same order.
